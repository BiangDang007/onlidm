The idea

onliDM is a focused browser for the direct-message inbox you already use. Feed, Reels, Explore, and other endless-scroll surfaces are blocked by onliDM's own URL rules, and the Stories tab clips the home page down to its stories row, so what is left is your conversations, stories and profiles.

No accounts, no servers, nothing tracked.

Questions

Is my login safe?+

Your login stays in your iPhone's WebKit storage, the same system Safari uses. onliDM has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, so there is nowhere for it to go.

Is this official?+

No. onliDM is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta, X Corp or LinkedIn. It opens their own websites inside a focused viewer.

What if a service changes its site?+

onliDM blocks pages like the feed or Explore using URL rules. If a service changes its site, those rules may need an update before blocking works again.

How do I sign out?+

Open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and tap "Sign out of all apps." That clears every login in one step.
