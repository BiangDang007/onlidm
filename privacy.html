Privacy policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

onliDM is a viewer for direct messages. This policy explains what that means for your data, in plain language.

What we collect

Nothing. onliDM has no user accounts, no backend servers, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect or see any of your messages, logins or usage activity.

What stays on your device

When you log in to Instagram, Messenger, X or LinkedIn inside onliDM, that session is held in your iPhone's WebKit storage, the same technology Safari uses, in a store that belongs only to onliDM. It lives only on your device. It is never sent to us, because onliDM has no server to send it to.

Two more things live only on your phone: the usernames you type for the Profile pages, and, if you use the Screen Time lock, the apps you picked. Apple hands onliDM those picks as opaque tokens that onliDM cannot read and never sends anywhere; onliDM keeps them on your phone and they go away when you delete onliDM.

The services you use inside onliDM

onliDM opens the real website of each service you choose. Whatever you do inside that session, such as reading messages or viewing a profile, is covered by that service's own privacy policy, not this one. Instagram and Messenger are operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., X by X Corp, and LinkedIn by LinkedIn Corporation.

Deleting your data

Deleting onliDM from your iPhone deletes every stored login immediately. Nothing is left anywhere else, since nothing was ever stored outside your device.

You can also clear every login without deleting the app: open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and tap "Sign out of all apps."

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
