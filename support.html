Getting started

Open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and turn on the services you use: Instagram, Messenger, X or LinkedIn. Log in to each one inside onliDM. Pick the network from that same tab; the bottom bar then shows Messages, Notifications and Profile for it, plus Stories on Instagram and Messenger.

Usernames for Profile pages

Instagram and X ask for your username the first time you open Profile. Type it once. It is kept on your phone and used only to open your own profile page. To change it, open the onliDM tab, edit it under "Usernames for Profile pages" and press return to save. Messenger and LinkedIn do not need one.

How to sign out

Open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and tap "Sign out of all apps." This clears every login held inside onliDM in one step. You will need to log in again the next time you open each service.

The Screen Time lock

Open the onliDM tab, tap "Lock the real apps" and switch the lock off at any time. Each day gives you two 5-minute passes. A pass counts down while onliDM is open. When you switch to the unlocked app, iOS pauses onliDM, so the lock returns the next time you open onliDM rather than exactly 5 minutes later. The lock needs Screen Time permission, which iOS asks for once.

How to show a hidden app again

Open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and toggle the service back on. It reappears in the network list right away.

Troubleshooting

If a service looks broken or shows a blank page, try closing and reopening onliDM, or signing out and back in. onliDM relies on each service's own website, so a redesign on their end can occasionally change how it looks inside the app.

FAQ

Is onliDM free?

Yes, onliDM is free in v1.

What devices does it support?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Which services can I use?

Instagram, Messenger, X and LinkedIn.

Does onliDM read my messages?

No. onliDM has no servers, accounts or analytics. It opens each service's own website and never sees what happens inside it.

Will onliDM notify me about new messages?

No. onliDM has no servers, so it cannot send push notifications. The Notifications tab opens each service's own notifications page.
