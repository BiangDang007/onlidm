Getting started

Open onliDM and log in to Instagram inside the app, the way you normally would. The bottom bar shows Messages, Stories, Notifications and Profile. The onliDM tab holds the lock, your Profile username and sign out.

Username for your Profile page

The Profile tab asks for your Instagram username the first time you open it. Type it once. It is kept on your phone and used only to open your own profile page. To change it, open the onliDM tab, edit it under "Username for your Profile page" and press return to save.

How to sign out

Open the onliDM tab at the bottom right and tap "Sign out of Instagram." That clears your login in one step. You will need to log in again the next time you open onliDM.

The Screen Time lock

Open the onliDM tab, tap "Lock the real apps" and switch the lock off at any time. Each day gives you two 5-minute passes. A pass counts down while onliDM is open. When you switch to the unlocked app, iOS pauses onliDM, so the lock returns the next time you open onliDM rather than exactly 5 minutes later. The lock needs Screen Time permission, which iOS asks for once.

Troubleshooting

If Instagram looks broken or shows a blank page, try closing and reopening onliDM, or signing out and back in. onliDM relies on Instagram's own website, so a redesign on Instagram's end can occasionally change how it looks inside the app.

FAQ

Is onliDM free?

Yes, onliDM is free in v1.

What devices does it support?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Which services can I use?

Version 1.0 works with Instagram.

Does onliDM read my messages?

No. onliDM has no servers, accounts or analytics. It opens Instagram's own website and never sees what happens inside it.

Will onliDM notify me about new messages?

No. onliDM has no servers, so it cannot send push notifications. The Notifications tab opens Instagram's own notifications page.
